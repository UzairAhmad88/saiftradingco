# Saif Trading Co — SEO Content Strategy & Internal Linking Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The content strategy for **Saif Trading Co** establishes sustainable topical authority around natural rough gemstones in Hong Kong, focusing exclusively on the company's verified business specializations:
- **Rough Tourmaline** (*Elbaite / Cyclosilicate*)
- **Rough Kunzite** (*Spodumene / Inosilicate*)
- **Rough Morganite** (*Beryl / Cyclosilicate*)

Rather than pursuing deceptive SEO tricks (keyword stuffing, thin doorway pages, AI spam, or fabricated reviews), the strategy couples **technically sound information architecture** with **human-readable, factually disciplined mineralogical content** and **purposeful internal linking**.

## 4. Keyword Cannibalization Rules

1. **Category vs. Guide Distinction:**
   - `/collections/tourmaline` is strictly commercial: aims for acquisition, trade terms, and specimen exploration ("Rough Tourmaline Crystals | Saif Trading Co").
   - `/education/tourmaline-guide` is strictly informational: aims for geological formation, crystallography, and optical behavior ("Tourmaline Crystal Morphology & Optical Properties").
   - Neither page copies or mirrors the other's title or main heading.
2. **Certification Policy vs. Testing Guide:**
   - `/certification` details the company's testing protocol, customer request procedure, and verification checklist.
   - `/education/certification-guide` details analytical gemological science (FTIR spectroscopy, Raman molecular fingerprinting, refractometry).
3. **No Duplicate Color/Size Permutations:**
   - No separate thin pages created for "Green Tourmaline Supplier", "Pink Tourmaline Supplier", or "50 Carat Tourmaline". All variations are handled through high-performance on-page facets that canonicalize to `/collections/tourmaline`.

## 6. Orphan Page & Broken Link Prevention Rules

1. **Zero Orphan Page Guarantee:**
   - Every published educational article is reachable through:
     1. The Education Hub landing page (`/education`).
     2. At least two related articles via `RelatedArticles`.
     3. The corresponding category page via `RelatedEducation`.
     4. The matching individual gemstone detail pages via `RelatedEducation`.
2. **Broken Link Safeguards:**
   - When a gemstone status changes from `available` to `sold`, its URL remains valid with an archival `sold` status badge. Links never break.
   - When a gemstone is marked `hidden` or `draft`, public queries strictly filter it out, ensuring it is never injected into `RelatedGemstonesSection` or sitemaps.
   - All collection-to-education links utilize verified programmatic slugs mapped to existing records in `EDUCATION_ARTICLES`.

## 7. Content Quality & Writing Discipline

### Human Writing Tone:
- Calm, scientific, concise, and respectful of professional gemologists, collectors, and mineral dealers.
- **Prohibited Clichés:** Never use "Whether you're a seasoned collector...", "Unlock the world of...", "Delve into...", "Ultimate guide to...", or "Everything you need to know...".

### Truthful Business Boundaries:
- Mention Hong Kong address (`417 Flat 4 Floor, Block B, Focal Industrial Centre, 21 Man Lok Street, Hung Hom, Kowloon`) and verified phone/email channels.
- **Zero Fabrication:** Never claim "direct mine ownership", "largest inventory in Asia", "certified gemologist on staff", or "guaranteed untreated stones" without verifiable physical documentation.

## 8. Publishing & Technical Revalidation Workflow

1. **Authoring / Editing:** Content authored with structured headings (`h2`, `h3`), descriptive alt text, reading times, and verified mineralogical data.
2. **Taxonomy Placement:** Assigned to one of four core categories: `Mineral Guides`, `Buying & Evaluation`, `Verification & Standards`, or `Specimen Handling`.
3. **Cross-Link Integration:** Link to at least 2 relevant educational guides and assign `relatedCollectionSlug` if mineral-specific.
4. **Verification & Build:** Run `npm run typecheck` and `npm run lint` to guarantee clean typing and absence of dead references.
5. **Cache Revalidation:** Next.js revalidation refreshes `/education`, `/education/[slug]`, and `/collections/[category]` automatically upon content modification.
