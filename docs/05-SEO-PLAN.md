# SEO Plan
Dynamic metadata, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots.txt, OpenGraph, JSON-LD, Organization/WebSite/Breadcrumb/Product schema where appropriate, semantic HTML, alt text, clean URLs, internal linking and optimized images.

Examples:
/collections/ruby
/collections/sapphire
/gemstones/2-14ct-burmese-ruby

Create educational content around ruby, sapphire, emerald, treatments, certification, origins, natural vs synthetic, buying guidance and care.

SEO optimization cannot honestly guarantee a #1 Google ranking.


## Primary Commercial Topics
Prioritize truthful search-focused content around:
- Rough gemstones
- Tourmaline
- Kunzite
- Morganite
- Rough Tourmaline supplier
- Rough Kunzite supplier
- Rough Morganite supplier

Do not make unsupported claims such as "largest", "best", "certified", "direct mine", "wholesale leader" or similar unless the client provides evidence.
